# Native app screenshots

The iPhone dialler and the Apple Watch app are native, so they can't be captured from a web build like the other
screens. Put their screenshots here, with exactly these names (PNG, JPG or WebP):

- `iphone-dialler.png` - an iPhone screenshot of the dialler during a call, with the caller card (name, file and stage)
  on screen. Portrait, straight from the device: 1179 x 2556 (iPhone 15/16/17 Pro) or 1290 x 2796 (Pro Max).
- `apple-watch.png` - an Apple Watch screenshot of an incoming call (or the calls-due list). Straight from the watch:
  416 x 496 (45 mm), 410 x 502 (Series 9 45 mm) or 422 x 514 (46 mm) are all fine.

Until they are added, the Mobile apps page describes these two apps in words only (no drawings).

The Mobile apps page picks them up automatically on the next build and shows them in a device frame in place of the
drawings. Use sample data only (no real clients or phone numbers). Plain screenshots straight from the device are
fine; keep each under about 500 KB.
